How this works

Each year, your contribution is added at the start, the portfolio grows by the gross return, and the management fee is deducted from the year-end balance. The difference between the two scenarios reflects both the direct fee drag and the compounding loss of returns on dollars paid in fees. The model assumes a constant rate of return — actual market returns vary year to year, which can amplify or dampen the gap. Taxes, withdrawals, inflation, and behavioral factors are not modeled.

For educational purposes only. Not personalized investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Actual outcomes depend on market returns, taxes, behavior, and other factors not modeled here. The fees shown for "Higher-Fee Option" reflect a typical advisor management fee — many advisors deliver value that justifies their fee through tax management, behavioral coaching, planning, and asset location. The point of this tool is to make the cost of fees visible, not to argue against advice.
