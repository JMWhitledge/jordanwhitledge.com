The long view

What $1,000 did across 98 years of markets

One thousand dollars in the S&P 500 at the start of 1928 grew to about $11.6 million by the end of 2025, with every dividend reinvested and every crash sat through. The arithmetic is simple. Sitting through the crashes is the hard part.
