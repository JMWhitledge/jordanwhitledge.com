Speaking

Presentations that change how people think about money.

I speak to organizations, associations, and universities. Every session is educational, evidence-based, and built for the audience in the room.

Talk 01

Recreating Your Paycheck in Retirement

For pre-retirees and employer groups. How to turn a lifetime of savings into steady income: Social Security timing, withdrawal order, and a plan for the years the market does not cooperate. Also offered as a focused Social Security session, and through University of Evansville continuing education.

Talk 02

The Advisor's Alpha: Where Real Financial Value Comes From

For professional associations, HR teams, and conferences. What the research says good advice is actually worth, and where that value shows up: planning, tax decisions, and steady guidance through hard markets, not stock picking.

Talk 03

The Unforced Error: Why Discipline Beats Intelligence in Investing

For corporate audiences, conferences, and universities. Why smart people make avoidable mistakes with money, and the simple structures that prevent them.

Custom Sessions

Available on lifecycle portfolio design, informed by my research in CFA Institute's Enterprising Investor, plus tax-efficient withdrawals, Social Security, and making decisions in uncertain economies.
