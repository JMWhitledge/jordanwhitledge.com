Typography

Playfair Display — Headings

Weight 500 (medium). Used for H1–H4, pull quotes, book titles, section headers.

DM Sans — Body, UI, Buttons

Weight 300–600. Body text at 400, buttons at 500, labels at 600. Line height: 1.7 for body, 1.15 for headings.

Buttons

Primary: Gold background (#C5A55A), navy text (#1B2A4A), 3px border radius, DM Sans 500, 0.88rem. Hover: #E8D5A3.

Secondary: Transparent background, 1px white/navy border, 3px radius. Hover: border turns gold.

Design Patterns

Section labels: DM Sans, 0.72rem, weight 500, letter-spacing 0.2em, uppercase, gold color.

Gold accent line: 48px wide, 2px tall, gold. Used below section titles.

Card style: Warm gray background, 4px radius, 3px gold top border on hover, subtle lift shadow.

Quote style: Playfair Display italic, gold-light color, 2px gold left border with 20px padding.

Positioning

Name: Jordan M. Whitledge (always include middle initial)

Credentials: CFA® charterholder, CFP® professional, MBA

Tone: Calm, confident, clear. Educator-first. Evidence over hype. Warm but professional.

Compliance note: Site must position as education and thought leadership — never direct client solicitation. Use "professional inquiries" not "become a client." Prospects self-select and initiate contact.
